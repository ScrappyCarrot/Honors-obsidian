## Task description

### 1. Where to find "The Truth" (Documented Data)

- **Official Permits (Overheid.nl):** Before a building is demolished, a **"Sloopmelding"** (Demolition Notice) must be filed. This always includes a **"Asbestinventarisatie"** (Asbestos inventory) and often a **"Stoffeninventarisatie"** (Material inventory).
    - *Search Strategy:* Go to [Overheid.nl](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.overheid.nl), search for "Bekendmakingen," filter by "Eindhoven," and use keywords like "Sloop" or the specific street name (e.g., "Fellenoord").
- **Oogstkaart (Harvest Map):** Check [Oogstkaart.nl](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.oogstkaart.nl). This is a Dutch marketplace where demolition contractors list materials *before* they are removed. You can see what's coming out of buildings in the Eindhoven region in real-time.
- **Madaster:** This is the national registry for **Material Passports**. While the full database is often behind a paywall, searching "Madaster Eindhoven case study" will show you reports on buildings being "mined" for materials in KnoopXL.
