# EXPECTATIONS AND MOTIVATION

-        What is the quality we are going for? What grade would make us happy?

-        What does every team member want to learn from this course?

-        What qualities and skills are present in this group?

-        What are the learning goals of each groupmember?

# ROLES, TASKS AND LEADERSHIP

-        Which roles are there to divide within the team?

-        Who will be doing what? Which tasks?

-        Who will take on what based on learning goals instead of strengths? Which roles and tasks best fit these goals?

# TEAM MEETINGS

-        How often will the team meet?

-        What will be the goal of these meetings?

-        Do we need a chairperson for these meetings? And how will this person be appointed?

-        Who will be taking minutes of these meetings so things like task division, action points, and conclusions don’t go missing?

# PLANNING

-        When do we come together with the team? Try to be specific and think about individual calendars.

-        Where at TU/e are we going to meet physically? Or will we meet online? /

-        Are we going to use a project planning and/or planning tools and what will be in it?

# COMMUNICATION WITHIN THE TEAM

-        How will the team document their work?

-        How will we communicate when not working together face to face?

# TEAM RULES AND EVALUATION

-        Which team agreements do we want to set for our team?

-        How do we deal with team members that don’t honor our team agreements?

-        How are we going to give each other personal feedback on our collaboration on a regular basis?

-        How do we celebrate our success?
