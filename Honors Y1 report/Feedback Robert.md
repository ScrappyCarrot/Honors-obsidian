**🔑 Core Feedback Summary (What matters most)**

**1. Start simple — don’t jump into design**

- First: **What is the problem?**
- Then: **Why does it matter for Eindhoven?**
- Only then: **Your solution**

Right now you’re starting too complex.

---

**2. Frame the real problem properly**

This is NOT just about a bridge.

👉 It’s about:

- **20–25 years of construction**
- **People growing up in disruption**
- **Loss of key connections (Kruisstraat tunnel)**
- **Impact on 372+ small businesses + residents**

➡️ **You are not building a bridge**  
➡️ **You are solving living in a construction city**

---

**3. Your concept = 3 clear values**

Stop trying to sell everything. Focus on:

**1. Functional**

- Keeps people moving (pedestrians + cyclists)

**2. Experiential**

- Viewpoints, identity, something people enjoy

**3. Social**

- Meeting space, safety, connection

👉 Everything you say should fit into one of these.

---

**4. Modularity = your strongest argument**

This is BIG. Use it better.

- Moves with construction phases
- Reused after KnoopXL
- Not a one-time cost
- Flexible, adaptable

➡️ This is what makes your idea **credible + smart**

---

**5. Municipality doesn’t care about details (yet)**

They want:

- Why this matters
- What it looks like
- Why it’s worth it

NOT:

- detailed construction logic
- rod dimensions
- technical complexity

---

**6. Use assumptions (important)**

You need numbers, even if estimated:

Example:

- “We estimate ~30–40% of current users will adopt this route”
- Based on:

- safety
- directness
- experience

➡️ Shows you are thinking realistically

---

**7. Position it as VALUE — not cost**

Big mindset shift:

❌ “It costs money”  
✅ “It provides value to the city”

Compare to:

- Sydney Opera House
- Initially criticized → now iconic

👉 Your bridge = **infrastructure + urban experience**

---

**8. Strong narrative needed**

Right now missing:

👉 A clear story like:

- “Eindhoven will be under construction for 25 years”
- “We give something back to the people”
- “We turn disruption into experience”
